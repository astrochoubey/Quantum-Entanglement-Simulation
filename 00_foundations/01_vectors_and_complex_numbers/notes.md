- To find the modulus of \(z = 3 + 4i\):
  1. Square the real part: \(3^2 = 9\)
  2. Square the imaginary part: \(4^2 = 16\)
  3. Add them together and take the square root: $\sqrt{9 + 16}$ = $\sqrt{25}$ = 5
